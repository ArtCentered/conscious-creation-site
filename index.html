Regulator. Meteorologist. Writer.

Travis Knudsen is the Executive Director of the Lane Regional Air Protection Agency in Oregon, where he leads the work of cleaning contaminated neighborhoods and helping rural communities breathe easier. Before that he spent a decade as a broadcast meteorologist, turning atmospheric chaos into something a family could plan a weekend around. In both jobs the work has been the same. Read a system nobody can see, and make it legible to the people living inside it.

He writes The Conscious Architect, a weekly newsletter, and is at work on a memoir of the same name. He lives in Eugene, Oregon, with his husband Michael and their cat Zeus. He paints on an iPad and writes at the kitchen table before the workday starts.

The Conscious Architect

A memoir, in progress

A former broadcast meteorologist and regional air-quality director traces a way of seeing back to where it began, in a childhood spent forecasting a house he could not control. The book is the account of what that life produced, and what it cost. Essays from the writing appear every Sunday in the newsletter.
